THE ROYAL INSTITUTION

The Dein of Agbor Kingdom

◆

The institution of the Dein stands at the heart of the traditional and cultural identity of Agbor Kingdom.

As the traditional ruler and custodian of the heritage of the Kingdom, the Dein represents continuity, identity, tradition and the enduring values of Agbor people.

The Royal Institution continues to serve as a bridge between generations, preserving the traditions of the Kingdom while providing leadership and guidance.
